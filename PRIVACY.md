# Reel privacy policy

_Last updated: October 31, 2026_

Reel (the website and the Android/iOS app) lets you browse movies, watch trailers, and see where films are available
to stream. This policy explains what happens to your data.

## What we collect

**Nothing personal.** Reel has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, and no analytics or tracking SDKs.

- **My List** is stored only on your device (browser local storage on the web, app storage on your phone). It is never
  sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing site data deletes it.
- **Search terms and movie lookups** are sent to The Movie Database (TMDB) API to fetch movie information. TMDB's
  privacy policy applies to those requests: https://www.themoviedb.org/privacy-policy
- **Trailers** open on YouTube, and "Where to watch" links open the streaming service's site. Those services have
  their own privacy policies.
- **Images** are loaded from TMDB's image servers, which can see your IP address like any website you visit.

## Children

Reel is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from them.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
